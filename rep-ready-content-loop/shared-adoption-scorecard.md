# Shared Adoption Scorecard

Part of the [rep-ready content loop](SKILL.md). Sets who owns what with sales leadership, and the numbers both teams watch.

Agree with sales leadership on two or three usage numbers, such as the share of deals with the right asset attached or the share of calls using the new talk track. Sales leaders then own adoption, while PMM owns the content.

---

## Steps

### 1. Agree the split

Agree on this with sales leadership up front:

| PMM owns | Sales leadership owns |
|---|---|
| What the content says, keeping it accurate, where it lives, and the product accuracy check | Whether reps use it, coaching reps on it in deal reviews, and the monthly feedback slot or thread |

### 2. Pick two or three numbers

Choose numbers both teams can see and agree matter. Lead with an outcome, then add usage.

| Type | Example numbers | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Outcome (lead with this) | Win rate in deals that used the asset vs. deals that didn't | [Content-to-deal linker](content-to-deal-linker.md) |
| Usage | Share of competitive deals with the battlecard opened | Content tool, CRM |
| Usage | Share of discovery calls using the new talk track | [Call language tracker](../pmm-impact-tracker/call-language-tracker.md) |
| Usage | Share of reps who watched the launch training | Training tool |

### 3. Set targets together

Agree a target for each number with the sales leader, such as "70% of competitive deals with the battlecard opened by end of Q2."

### 4. Report monthly

Add one line to the monthly rollup in the impact tracker's [launch scorecard](../pmm-impact-tracker/launch-scorecard.md), outcome first:

> **Adoption, March:** Deals that used the Competitor X card won 46% vs. 29% (24 and 31 deals). The card was opened in 62% of competitive deals (target 70%). West team at 38%; their manager is coaching on it this month.

### 5. Review quarterly, together

PMM and the sales leader look at the numbers together for 30 minutes each quarter. Put it on an existing sales leadership meeting's agenda rather than booking a separate meeting. This keeps adoption from quietly becoming PMM's job again.

**Agenda:**
1. **The numbers,** outcome first.
2. **If a target missed, diagnose together before blaming anyone.** Look at the [content finder audit](content-finder-audit.md) (can reps find it?) and the [field feedback loop](field-feedback-loop.md) (what are reps saying?) side by side. Decide whether it's a content, placement, or coaching problem.
3. **Keep, fix, move, or retire,** using the [content-to-deal linker](content-to-deal-linker.md) decisions, with an owner for each.
4. **Feature gaps:** the grouped feature-gap summary from the field feedback loop, sent to Product afterward.
5. **Coaching:** which manager is coaching which team.

### 6. Give leadership one line

When several teams have scorecards, roll them up into one line for leadership: the outcome number across all assets, with usage behind it.

> **Sales content, Q1:** Deals that used PMM's sales content won 41% vs. 30% for deals that didn't, across all segments. Usage: 64% of competitive deals had the right card opened (target 70%).

---

## CS and support version

Agree the same split with the CS or support leader. Outcome numbers become setup completion, repeat tickets, or resolution time in accounts that used the content vs. those that didn't. Usage numbers become the share of tickets on the topic where the macro or guide was used.

---

## Rules

- Lead with the outcome number, not opens and sends.
- No more than three numbers. More than that and no one watches any of them.
- Team-level numbers only; no individual rep rankings.
- If adoption is low, check where the content lives before blaming reps or the sales leader.
- If the sales leader stops coming to the quarterly review, raise it with your manager; the split only works if both sides hold up their part.

---
