# Rep Voice Rewrite

Part of the [rep-ready content loop](SKILL.md). Runs before any card or talk track ships, after the [10-second card builder](ten-second-card-builder.md).

Have a top rep rewrite the talk tracks in their own words before launch. AI and marketing drafts tend to sound like marketing, and reps won't say lines that don't sound like them.

---

## When to use it

- Any "You say," "What to write when you send it," or "Here's how" line.
- Talk tracks, demo scripts, discovery questions, and launch-day pitches.
- Any draft written with AI.

---

## Steps

### 1. Pick the rep

Choose a top performer in the segment the card is for. If the card is for newer reps too, add one newer rep as a second reader.

### 2. Read it out loud

Ask the rep to read each line out loud, as if to a buyer. Watch for where they stumble, pause, or change words without meaning to. Those are the lines to fix.

### 3. Ask three questions

1. "Would you actually say this? If not, how would you say it?"
2. "Is there a word here a buyer would roll their eyes at?"
3. "What would a buyer say back, and does the card help you answer?"

### 4. Rewrite in their words

Let the rep rewrite the line. Keep their wording unless it changes the meaning or makes a claim the product can't back up.

### 5. Recheck the facts

After the rewrite, the product checker confirms every feature claim is still true. Rep rewrites are where overpromises tend to creep in.

---

## Signs a line sounds like marketing

- Words reps never say on calls, like "leverage," "seamless," "robust," "best-in-class," or "unlock."
- Sentences longer than a rep can say in one breath.
- Claims with no number or example behind them.
- Lines that describe the product instead of answering what the buyer said.

---

## CS and support version

Use a CS manager or support agent who handles the most tickets on the topic. Read it as if replying to a customer.

---

## Rules

- No card ships without a rep voice check and a product accuracy check after it.
- Keep the rep's wording over marketing's unless it's inaccurate.
- Credit the rep who rewrote it on the card or in the asset register.
- Redo it when the card's main line changes.
