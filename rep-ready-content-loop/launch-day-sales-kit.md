# Launch-Day Sales Kit

Part of the [rep-ready content loop](SKILL.md). The loop applied to a launch: one bundle, checked and placed in the rep's workflow on day one.

For each launch, ship one bundle: a one-line pitch, three discovery questions, the top two objections with answers, one proof point, and a 10-minute training recording. It replaces the folder of ten PDFs nobody opens.

---

## When to use it

- Tier 1 and Tier 2 launches that sales needs to talk about.
- **Tier 3 or small updates:** a one-line Slack post with what changed and who to tell is enough.
- **Self-serve or CS launches:** use the CS version below.

**This kit is for sales and CS only.** The website, email, in-app, and press parts of the launch live in the full launch plan. Link to it from the kit so reps can see what customers will see.

---

## What goes in the kit

| Piece | What it is | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| One-line pitch | What it is, who it's for, and why it matters, in one sentence a rep would say | Passes the [rep voice rewrite](rep-voice-rewrite.md) |
| Three discovery questions | Questions that find buyers who need it | Each one should qualify, not just start a chat |
| Top two objections | "When they say / You say" for the two most likely pushbacks | Built with the [10-second card builder](ten-second-card-builder.md) |
| One proof point | A number, beta result, or customer quote, with source | If there's no proof yet, say what to use instead, such as a demo |
| 10-minute training recording | A walkthrough a rep can watch before their next call | Ten minutes or less, with a demo |
| Who to talk to | Which accounts or segments to bring it up with first | A saved CRM list or filter, if possible |
| What's not ready | What reps shouldn't promise yet, written as "Don't promise X" without future dates | Agreed with Product; internal only |

## Steps

### Short notice (a week or less)

If Product gives less than two weeks' notice, ship the core on launch day and the rest by day 7:
- **Launch day:** the one-line pitch, the top objection, and "What's not ready."
- **By day 7:** the discovery questions, the second objection, the proof point, and the training recording.

### 1. Two weeks before launch

- Run a short [rep needs interview](rep-needs-interview.md) with two or three reps: "When a buyer hears about this, what will they ask first?"
- Draft the kit from the product brief and the impact plan.
- Get the product accuracy check and agree on "What's not ready."

### 2. One week before

- Run the [rep voice rewrite](rep-voice-rewrite.md) on the pitch and objection answers.
- Record the 10-minute training.
- Agree with the sales partner on where the kit will live, per the [content finder audit](content-finder-audit.md).

### 3. Launch day

- Put the whole kit in one place inside reps' workflow, such as a CRM page, a pinned Slack post, or the sales content tool, with one link.
- Post the announcement where reps already talk: one line on what it is, one on who to tell, and the link.
- Add the kit to the asset register in the [freshness cycle](freshness-cycle.md), with an owner and a review date.

### 4. Days 7 and 30

- **Next sales meeting:** A two-minute recap of the pitch and the top objection, so the training sticks.
- **Day 7:** Ask in the [field feedback loop](field-feedback-loop.md) what buyers are asking that the kit doesn't answer, and ask two reps to say the pitch out loud. If they can't, simplify it. Update the kit.
- **Day 30:** Check usage and the early signs in the impact tracker's [launch scorecard](../pmm-impact-tracker/launch-scorecard.md).

```
  "___"

  WHEN THEY SAY: "___"

  WHEN THEY SAY: "___"
  YOU SAY: "___"

PROOF POINT: ___ (source: ___)

TRAINING RECORDING: [link] (__ minutes)

WHO TO TALK TO FIRST: [segment or saved list]

WHAT'S NOT READY (internal only — "Don't promise ___"): ___

FULL LAUNCH PLAN: [link]

OWNER: ___ · PRODUCT CHECK: [PM name, date] · REP VOICE CHECK: [rep name, date]
```

---

## CS and support version

Swap the discovery questions for "who to tell" (which accounts benefit), the objections for the two most likely customer questions (as CS cards), and the pitch for one line a CS manager can put in a check-in email. Put it in the help desk or CS platform, and announce it in the CS channel.

---

## Rules

- One kit, one link. No folders of separate PDFs.
- Everything in the kit passes the rep voice check and the product accuracy check.
- Ten minutes of training or less.
- Say what's not ready, so reps don't overpromise, and keep that section internal. Write "Don't promise X," never future dates.
- The kit gets an owner and a review date like any other asset.
