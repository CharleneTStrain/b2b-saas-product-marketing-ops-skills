# 10-Second Card Builder

Part of the [rep-ready content loop](SKILL.md). Step 2 of the loop: build it so it can be used in 10 seconds.

Turn any battlecard, one-pager, or objection guide into something a rep can scan in 10 seconds. Cut anything that doesn't change what the rep says or sends next.

Start from the brief in the [rep needs interview](rep-needs-interview.md).

---

## Rep-facing card (for content reps read)

```
WHEN THEY SAY: [the buyer's real words]
YOU SAY: [one or two sentences, in a rep's voice]
PROOF: [up to three facts, numbers, or customer examples, each with a source]
ASK: [one question that moves the deal forward]
DON'T SAY: [the line that backfires, if there is one]
LAST UPDATED: [date] · OWNER: [name] · PRODUCT CHECK: [PM name, date]
```

**Example:**

```
WHEN THEY SAY: "X's dashboards look a lot more polished."
YOU SAY: "They do look great. The question is how long it takes your team
  to build one. Ours takes about five minutes, with no analyst needed."
PROOF: Acme's ops team cut weekly reporting from 4 hours to 20 minutes
  (Acme case study, 2026).
ASK: "Who on your team builds reports today, and how long does it take them?"
DON'T SAY: "Their dashboards are just pretty." Buyers hear it as dismissive.
LAST UPDATED: Feb 10 · OWNER: [PMM name] · PRODUCT CHECK: [PM name], Feb 9
```

---

## Buyer-facing card (for content reps send)

Case studies, ROI tools, one-pagers, and comparison pages get a cover note the rep sees before sending:

```
SEND IT WHEN: [the moment, in the buyer's words]
WHO IT'S FOR: [buyer role and segment]
THE ONE THING IT PROVES: [one sentence]
WHAT TO WRITE WHEN YOU SEND IT: [two or three sentences the rep can copy]
LAST UPDATED: [date] · OWNER: [name] · PRODUCT CHECK: [PM name, date]
```

The asset itself should make its main point on the first screen or page, so a busy buyer gets it without reading the whole thing.

## CS and support card

```
WHEN THE CUSTOMER ASKS: [their real words]
HERE'S HOW: [the answer, in one or two sentences, in a CS manager's voice]
STEPS OR LINK: [numbered steps, or a link to the help article]
ESCALATE WHEN: [the condition, and who to escalate to]
LAST UPDATED: [date] · OWNER: [name] · PRODUCT CHECK: [PM name, date]
```

**Example:**

```
WHEN THE CUSTOMER ASKS: "Why isn't my data syncing from the integration?"
HERE'S HOW: "Most sync issues come from a permission that wasn't granted during
  setup. It takes about two minutes to fix."
STEPS OR LINK: 1. Settings → Integrations → [App]. 2. Click "Reconnect."
  3. Approve the "read data" permission. Help article: [link]
ESCALATE WHEN: Reconnecting doesn't fix it within an hour, or the error says
  "account locked." Escalate to Tier 2 support.
LAST UPDATED: Mar 4 · OWNER: [PMM name] · PRODUCT CHECK: [PM name], Mar 3
```

## Turning an old asset into a card

1. Find the one moment it's for. If it covers several, plan one card per moment.
2. Pull out the buyer's objection or question, in their words.
3. Keep the one or two sentences a rep would actually say back.
4. Keep up to three proof points that have a source.
5. Add one question to ask next.
6. Move everything else (pricing history, feature lists, funding rounds, executive bios) into PMM's notes.

---

## Checks before it ships

1. **Rep voice check:** Run the [rep voice rewrite](rep-voice-rewrite.md).
2. **Product accuracy check:** The product checker confirms every feature claim is true today, after the rep's rewrite. Anything planned but not shipped gets cut or marked "coming in [quarter]" with Product's agreement.

---

## Rules

- One screen, no scrolling. If it doesn't fit, it's two cards.
- If a line doesn't change what the rep says or sends next, cut it.
- Every proof point needs a source a rep can name if a buyer asks.
- Include an honest "where they're stronger" line on competitive cards. Reps trust cards that admit a weakness.
- No card ships without both checks.
