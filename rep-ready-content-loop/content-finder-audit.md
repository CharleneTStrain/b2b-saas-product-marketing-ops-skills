# Content Finder Audit

Part of the [rep-ready content loop](SKILL.md). Step 4 of the loop: put content where reps already are.

List every sales asset, where it lives, and how long it takes a rep to reach it during a live call. Then move the most-used ones into the tools reps already have open. Where content lives matters more than how good it is.

---

## When to use it

- Once when you start, to see what you already have.
- Every time you ship a new asset, using the click test.
- Whenever an asset has low use, before rewriting it.

---

## Steps

### 1. List every asset

Pull everything reps might use from every place it lives: the content tool, shared drives, the CRM, Slack pins, the wiki, and old launch folders.

| Asset | Where it lives (every place) | Copies | Last updated | Uses last 90 days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor X battlecard | Drive folder, Highspot, old Slack pin | 3 | 8 months ago | 4 |

### 2. Run the click test

For the top 10 to 20 assets, ask a rep to find each one during a mock call while you time it. Note the number of clicks and seconds.

| Asset | Clicks | Seconds | Found it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor X battlecard | 6 | 75 | Yes, but the old copy |

**Rule:** If it takes more than 30 seconds, move it.

### 3. Move it where reps already are

In order of preference:
1. **Inside the tool reps have open in the moment,** such as a call recorder sidebar, a CRM opportunity field, or a deal-stage prompt.
2. **One search away,** in Slack with a pinned message or a bot, or in your sales content tool tagged by deal stage and competitor.
3. **One click from a bookmark,** as a single linked page, not a folder.

### 4. Clean up

- Pick one home per asset and delete or redirect every other copy.
- Tag every asset by deal stage, competitor, and segment, using the words reps searched for in the [rep needs interview](rep-needs-interview.md).
- Mark anything unused in 90 days for the retire review in the [freshness cycle](freshness-cycle.md).

### 5. Announce it

Post where reps already talk, with one line on the moment it's for and a link. Skip the email with a PDF attached.

> **New:** Competitor X reporting card, for when a buyer says X's dashboards look better. It's in the Gong sidebar and pinned in #sales-competitive.

---

## CS and support version

Look in the help desk or ticketing tool, the CS platform, saved replies or macros, and the help center. Run the click test with a CS manager or agent during a mock ticket or call.

---

## Rules

- One home per asset. If a card lives in three places, two of them will go out of date.
- Tag by how reps search, not by how marketing files things.
- Low use usually means bad placement, not bad content. Move it first, then rewrite.
- Redo the click test after any tool change, such as a new CRM layout or content tool.
