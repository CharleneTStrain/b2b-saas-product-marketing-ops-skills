# Freshness Cycle

Part of the [rep-ready content loop](SKILL.md). Step 5 of the loop: keep every asset accurate, and retire what isn't used.

Give every asset an owner and a review date, refresh competitive content weekly or after any competitor move, and stamp each card with its "last updated" date. Reps stop trusting content once they've been burned by outdated information.

---

## Refresh cadence

| Content | How often |
|---|---|
| Top two or three competitors | Weekly, and within two days of a launch, price change, or major news |
| Other competitors | Monthly |
| Product claims | Within five business days of any release that changes them, triggered by release notes |
| Everything else | Quarterly |

---

## Monthly check (first business day of each month)

| Check | Where to look | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Is it helping? | Win rate and deal speed in deals that used it, compared with deals that didn't, using the [content-to-deal linker](content-to-deal-linker.md) | Report this first; feed it into the PMM impact tracker |
| Is it being used? | Content tool opens and sends, CRM attachments, call searches for the "You say" lines | Low use: run the [content finder audit](content-finder-audit.md) before changing what it says |
| Is it accurate? | Competitor sites, release notes, pricing changes, win/loss notes | Update it, get a new product check if claims changed, and change the "last updated" date |
| What are reps saying? | The [field feedback loop](field-feedback-loop.md) | Act on each request |

---

## Statuses

- **Proposed:** suggested, but not yet agreed as PMM's to build. Name who decides and when. Don't start building until it's agreed.
- **Not started:** agreed, not yet built.
- **In progress (due: ___):** being built now. Give the due date.
- **Blocked (on: ___):** can't move until something else happens, such as a claims answer or a product decision. Name what it's waiting on and who owns that.
- **On hold:** paused on purpose. Note why and when to revisit.
- **Live:** current and in use.
- **Fix:** live but needs an update.
- **Retire:** archived.

---

## Rules

- **Every asset has an owner and a next review date.** No owner means it gets retired.
- **Every card shows its "last updated" date,** so reps can see it's current.
- **Retire anything unused for 90 days,** unless it's for a rare but important moment, like a security review. Archive it; don't leave it sitting there.
- **Low use usually means bad placement,** not bad content. Move it first, then rewrite.
- **Stop rule:** If an asset still isn't used 60 days after being moved and rewritten, retire it, and stop building that type of asset for that moment until reps ask for it.
- **When a competitor moves,** update the card within two days and post a one-line note where reps talk: what changed and what to say now.
