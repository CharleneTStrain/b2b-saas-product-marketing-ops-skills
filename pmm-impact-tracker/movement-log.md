# Movement Log

Part of the [PMM impact tracker](SKILL.md). Step 2 of the loop: record signs the work is landing while it's live.

Each week, record the movement that dashboards miss: a rep using the new message on a call, a deal citing a new proof point, Product changing a roadmap item because of your research. This is how upstream work gets on the record.

**Who reads it:** The log is a working file for PMM and your manager. Leadership sees what comes out of it in the monthly rollup in the [launch scorecard](launch-scorecard.md) and in the [quarterly impact story](quarterly-impact-story.md), not the log itself.

---

## Log an entry

Add an entry whenever you see a sign. Spend five minutes a week, on the same day each week.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | Feb 12 |
| What happened | Rep on the Acme deal used the "reporting in 5 minutes" line, and the buyer asked for a second demo |
| Type | Message used / Deal moved / Deal lost / Funnel moved / Product used / Product changed / Leader decision / Outside factor / Tracking change |
| Source | Link to the Gong call |
| Deal value (sales-led only) | $48K, or "—" for self-serve |
| Linked to which early sign | Sign 1: reps using the message |

## Where to look each week

Don't check every source every week. In the [impact plan](impact-plan.md), choose the two or three sources that matter most for this project, and check those weekly. Check the rest once a month. The list below covers the options.

- **Call recordings:** Search for 2–3 exact phrases from the new messaging. The [call language tracker](call-language-tracker.md) turns this into counts.
- **CRM:** Check deals won or lost against the main competitor, and the reason given for each loss. The [win/loss revenue tagger](win-loss-revenue-tagger.md) turns these into real reasons with dollar values.
- **Content tool:** See which assets were sent, and in which deals. The [content-to-deal linker](content-to-deal-linker.md) turns this into a comparison.
- **Funnel and product analytics:** Check each funnel stage you're tracking, and whether usage holds steady.
- **In-app surveys, support, and CS tickets:** Look for questions, confusion, or praise about the feature or message.
- **Slack and email:** Look for reps or CS asking questions about the topic, or sharing wins.
- **Product and leadership meetings:** Note any decision that cited your research or messaging.
- **Outside the company:** Note holidays, partner launches, competitor moves, or other teams' promotions as they happen.

---

## Rules

- Log every loss and drop you see, not only wins. A log of highlights is just a list of anecdotes; leadership trusts a log that shows both.
- At the end of each month, count the entries by type (for example, "9 message used, 4 deals moved, 3 deals lost, 1 outside factor"). The counts show the full picture, and a month with only good news is a sign something is missing.
- For "Product changed" and "Leader decision" entries, write "cited PMM research" and link the decision. Don't write "PMM drove" or "because of PMM"; other teams make their own decisions.
- For every lost deal, write down the real reason, not just the CRM dropdown choice, along with the dollar value.
- When sharing loss data with Product, group it across many deals ("6 losses, $210K, same reason"). One big deal is a story, not a pattern.
- Log outside factors when they happen, even if you named them in the [impact plan](impact-plan.md), so the timing is on record.
- Log tracking changes, such as a new event going live, a broken event, or a definition change, so later numbers aren't misread.
- If two weeks go by with no entries, that's a finding in itself: either the work isn't landing or you aren't looking in the right places.
