# Call Language Tracker

Part of the [PMM impact tracker](SKILL.md). Feeds the "Message used" entries in the [movement log](movement-log.md) and the message-on-calls early sign in the [launch scorecard](launch-scorecard.md).

Search call recordings for the exact phrases from the messaging, and report how often reps and buyers repeat them before and after a launch. It's the clearest sign that a new message is actually reaching the market.

---

## When to use it

- After a messaging refresh, positioning change, or launch with new talk tracks.
- When leadership asks whether reps are "actually using the new messaging."
- **Self-serve:** use the same steps on support tickets, chat transcripts, survey answers, and reviews instead of calls.

---

## Steps

### 1. Pick the phrases

Choose three to five exact phrases from the new messaging, plus the old phrases they replace.

| New phrase | Old phrase it replaces | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| "reporting in 5 minutes" | "powerful reporting" | Core proof point for the launch |
| "no analyst needed" | "easy to use" | The main difference from Competitor X |

- Pick phrases short enough that people actually say them. A full value proposition won't show up word for word.
- Include close variations ("five minutes," "5 min") so you don't undercount.
- Write them down before launch. Choosing phrases after you've seen the results invites cherry-picking.

### 2. Get a starting point

Count each phrase in calls from the 30 to 60 days before launch. Use the same call types you'll measure afterward, such as discovery and demo calls in the target segment.

### 3. Count after launch

At each check-in, count again over the same length of time. Track three things separately:

| What to count | Why |
|---|---|
| **Rep says the new phrase** | Shows the training landed |
| **Buyer says the new phrase back** | The strongest sign: the message is sticking with the market |
| **Rep still says the old phrase** | Shows where the old message is hanging on |

Split the counts by team, region, or segment, so you can see who needs help.

### 4. Report it

Report it as a share of calls, not raw counts, since call volume changes.

> **Message on calls, day 60:** "Reporting in 5 minutes" came up in 45% of mid-market discovery and demo calls, up from 3% before launch. Buyers repeated it in 12% of calls. The old "powerful reporting" line dropped from 38% to 9%. The West team is at 20% and needs a refresher.

---

## Rules

- **Use at least 20 calls per period** before reporting a share. With fewer, say "too few calls to tell yet."
- **Count buyers separately from reps.** A rep repeating a phrase shows training. A buyer repeating it shows the message is landing.
- **Compare like with like:** the same call types, segment, and length of time before and after.
- **Use the counts to coach, not to police.** Share team-level results with sales managers; don't rank individual reps publicly.
- **Pull a few real quotes** of buyers using the language for the [quarterly impact story](quarterly-impact-story.md). A single good quote often lands better than the percentage.
- **If the call tool's search misses variations,** spot-check 10 calls by hand and note the gap.
