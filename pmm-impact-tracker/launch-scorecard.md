# Launch Scorecard

Part of the [PMM impact tracker](SKILL.md). Steps 3 and 4 of the loop: the monthly rollup across all projects, and the day 30, 60, and 90 checks against the targets in the [impact plan](impact-plan.md).

At each check, report adoption, pipeline touched, win rate against key competitors, and sales questions answered, compared with the targets you set up front.

---

## Monthly rollup (across all projects)

On the first business day of each month, starting with the first month that has data, send leadership one short update covering every active project.

> **PMM impact, March (sales-led):** 4 projects live. PMM work was used in deals worth $1.2M in open pipeline. Deals that used it won 9 points more often than deals that didn't. On track: 3. Behind: 1 (pricing page, low usage; fix in progress).

> **PMM impact, March (self-serve):** 3 projects live. Setup completion for users who saw the new onboarding rose from 42% to 51%; the comparison group held at 43%. On track: 2. Behind: 1 (upgrade page; tracking event ships Mar 18, so no data yet).

Then add one line per project with its main number and early signs, marked as on track or behind. That's it.

### Day 30: Is the problem the product or the message?

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| People hear the message and are interested, but don't use the feature | Product | Share the usage data with Product, and pause new marketing until it's fixed |
| People who find the feature use it, but few find it (reps don't mention it, or users don't see it) | Message delivery | Retrain reps, move the asset into tools people already use, or move the in-product surface |
| People see the message and aren't interested | Message | Go back to customer interviews and rewrite |
| Nothing moving anywhere | Too early, the wrong target, or tracking isn't working | Check the "Tracking change" entries in the [movement log](movement-log.md), then talk to your manager about whether to keep going |

### How to read the main number

- **Early signs moving, main number flat:** It's probably too soon, so keep going.
- **Early signs flat:** The message or asset isn't reaching people. Fix how it's delivered before rewriting it.
- **Main number moving, early signs flat:** Something else is driving the change. Check the outside factors you named and logged, and say so honestly; don't claim it.

When another team also touched the result, use the [shared-metric splitter](shared-metric-splitter.md) before reporting it.
