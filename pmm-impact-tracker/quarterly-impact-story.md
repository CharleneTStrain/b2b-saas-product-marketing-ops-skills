# Quarterly Impact Story

Part of the [PMM impact tracker](SKILL.md). Step 5 of the loop: turn the [movement log](movement-log.md) and [launch scorecards](launch-scorecard.md) into a one-page readout.

It answers "how do you know it's working?" before anyone asks.

---

## Write the readout

This goes to your manager, the CMO, or the CEO. Make it the standard PMM update at quarterly business reviews, so it becomes routine rather than a one-off.

1. **Why it mattered:** The company goal it supported.
2. **What we set out to change:** One sentence, plus the target and whose target it is.
3. **What happened:** The main number from start to now, whether you hit the target, and usage.
4. **Proof:** Three movement log entries with links. For sales-led work, use one call, one deal, and one decision. For self-serve work, use one funnel change, one piece of user feedback, and one decision.
5. **How sure we are:** The comparison you used, and how each outside factor named in the [impact plan](impact-plan.md) or logged in the movement log may have helped or hurt. Use the wording from the [shared-metric splitter](shared-metric-splitter.md).
6. **What didn't work:** One honest miss and what you changed because of it.
7. **What's next:** The next target, or what you're stopping.

For more than one project, write one short block per project, then add a top line: "This quarter, PMM work changed [three things]."

---

## Example: sales-led

> **Why:** Supports the company goal of growing mid-market revenue 25% this year.
>
> **Goal:** Win more mid-market deals against Competitor X on reporting (31% → 40% by end of Q2). Target set by PMM with the VP of Product Marketing.
>
> **Result:** 41% at day 90. Target hit. 33% of mid-market accounts now use the dashboard.
>
> **Proof:** (1) Acme call where the buyer asked for a second demo after hearing the new reporting line, $48K, won. (2) The "reporting gap" loss reason dropped from 4 deals to 1. (3) Product moved scheduled exports up a quarter after win/loss showed 6 losses worth $210K for the same reason.
>
> **How sure we are:** We rolled out to the East region first. East's win rate rose 10 points in that time; West rose 2. Competitor X raised prices in March, as we flagged in the plan, which likely helped both regions.
>
> **Miss:** The West team was slow to use the message. A 20-minute retraining at day 30 fixed it.
>
> **Next:** Same approach for enterprise, with a target of 25% → 33% against Competitor Y.

## Template

```
WHY: Supports [company goal].
GOAL: [The change] ([start] → [target] by [date]). Target set by [PMM with manager / owner].
RESULT: [Main number now]. [Hit / missed / on track]. [Usage].
PROOF:
  (1) [Call or funnel change, with link]
  (2) [Deal or user feedback, with link]
  (3) [Decision, with link]
HOW SURE WE ARE: [Comparison and result]. [Outside factors and their likely effect].
MISS: [What didn't work] — [what we changed].
NEXT: [Next target, or what we're stopping].
```
