# PMM Impact Tracker

**What it does:** It gives every PMM project a target before work starts, a running record of evidence while it's live, a monthly rollup for leadership, and a short readout at the end, so "how do you know it's working?" always has an answer.

**When to use it:**
- **Full version:** Tier 1 launches, messaging refreshes, pricing or packaging changes, and research or enablement programs that should change what Sales says or what buyers do.
- **Light version:** Tier 2 work. Do Step 1 and the day-90 check only.
- **Skip it:** Small updates that don't change what anyone says or does.

---

## Step 1: Set the target with your manager (before any work starts)

Fill in one plan per project, in 15 minutes or less, and agree on it together.

| Field | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Project | The name and one line on what it is | New reporting dashboard launch |
| Why this matters | The company goal it supports | Company goal: grow mid-market revenue 25% this year |
| The change we want | What should be different afterward, in one sentence | Mid-market buyers stop choosing Competitor X over us for reporting |
| Who has to change | The people whose behavior should shift | Mid-market reps, buyers in active deals, existing customers |
| Main number | One number that shows the change happened | Win rate against Competitor X in mid-market |
| Starting point | Where that number is today | 31% (last 2 quarters) |
| Target | Where it should be, and by when | 40% by end of Q2 |
| Early signs | 2–3 sales signs, plus 1 product-usage sign | Reps use the "reporting in 5 minutes" line on calls; buyers ask for the dashboard demo; fewer "reporting gap" loss reasons; **usage:** 30% of mid-market accounts open the dashboard within 60 days |
| Where the evidence lives | The exact tool for each sign | Gong for calls, Salesforce for deals and loss reasons, Highspot for asset sends, Pendo or Amplitude for usage |
| Comparison plan | How you'll tell whether PMM's work made the difference | Compare deals where the new demo was used against deals where it wasn't; if possible, roll out to one region first as a test group |
| Check-in dates | Days 30, 60, and 90, already on the calendar | Mar 1, Apr 1, May 1 |
| What PMM owns | Your part, as distinct from Sales' or Product's | The message, demo script, battlecard, and rep training |

**Rules for Step 1:**
- One main number only. If you can't pick one, the goal isn't clear yet.
- The starting point has to be real data, not a guess. If the data doesn't exist, the first task is to start collecting it.
- The target is set with your manager, not alone, so it isn't too easy or too hard.
- If you can't fill in "why this matters," question whether the project should happen at all.

### Template: impact plan

```
PROJECT:
WHY THIS MATTERS (company goal):

```

## Step 2: Log the evidence (while it's live)

Add an entry whenever you see a sign. Spend five minutes a week, on the same day each week.

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Date | Feb 12 |
| What happened | Rep on the Acme deal used the "reporting in 5 minutes" line, and the buyer asked for a second demo |
| Type | Message used / Deal moved / Deal lost / Product used / Product changed / Leader decision |
| Source | Link to the Gong call |
| Deal value, if any | $48K |
| Linked to which early sign | Sign 1: reps using the message |

**Where to look each week:**
- **Call recordings:** Search for 2–3 exact phrases from the new messaging.
- **CRM:** Check deals won or lost against the main competitor, and the reason given for each loss.
- **Content tool:** See which assets were sent, and in which deals.
- **Product analytics:** Check whether customers are using the feature, and whether usage holds steady.
- **Slack and email:** Look for reps asking questions about the topic, or sharing wins.
- **Product and leadership meetings:** Note any decision that cited your research or messaging.

**Rules for Step 2:**
- Log losses too, not only wins. Leadership trusts a log that shows both.
- For every lost deal, write down the real reason, not just the CRM dropdown choice, along with the dollar value.
- When sharing loss data with Product, group it across many deals ("6 losses, $210K, same reason"). One big deal is a story, not a pattern.
- If two weeks go by with no entries, that's a finding in itself: either the work isn't landing or you aren't looking in the right places.

## Step 3: Monthly rollup (across all projects)

On the first of each month, send leadership one short update covering every active project.

> **PMM impact, March:** 4 projects live. PMM work was used in deals worth $1.2M in open pipeline. Deals that used it won 9 points more often than deals that didn't. On track: 3. Behind: 1 (pricing page, low usage; fix in progress).

Then add one line per project with its main number and early signs, marked as on track or behind. That's it.

### Template: monthly rollup

```
PMM IMPACT, [MONTH]:
[#] projects live. PMM work was used in deals worth $[X] in open pipeline.
Deals that used it won [Y] points more often than deals that didn't.
On track: [#]. Behind: [#] ([project], [reason]; [fix]).

- [Project]: main number [start → now], early signs [summary] — on track / behind
- [Project]: ...
```

## Step 4: Check against the target (days 30, 60, 90)

| | Day 30 | Day 60 | Day 90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main number | 33% | 36% | 41% |
| Early sign 1: message on calls | 20% | 45% | 55% |
| Early sign 2: demo requests | 6 | 14 | 22 |
| Early sign 3: "reporting gap" losses | 4 | 2 | 1 |
| Usage: accounts opening the dashboard | 12% | 24% | 33% |
| On track? | Early, slow | Yes | Target hit |
| What I'm changing | Retrain the West team, whose usage lags | None | Expand to enterprise |

### Day 30: Is the problem the product or the message?

| What you see | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Reps use the message, buyers are interested, but customers don't use the feature | Product | Share the usage data with Product, and pause new marketing until it's fixed |
| Customers who find the feature use it, but reps don't mention it | Message delivery | Retrain reps or move the asset into the tools they already use |
| Reps mention it and buyers aren't interested | Message | Go back to customer interviews and rewrite |
| Nothing moving anywhere | Too early, or the wrong target | Check with your manager and decide whether to keep going |

### How to read the main number

- **Early signs moving, main number flat:** It's probably too soon, so keep going.
- **Early signs flat:** The message or asset isn't reaching people. Fix how it's delivered before rewriting it.
- **Main number moving, early signs flat:** Something else is driving the change. Say so honestly; don't claim it.

### Shared numbers

When Sales or Demand Gen also touched the result, compare deals that used your work against deals that didn't (win rate, deal size, days to close). Report it as "deals that used X closed Y% more often," not "PMM drove $Z."

Note that this shows a link, not proof: stronger reps may simply use more content. A test group, where one region or team gets the work first, is the strongest evidence when you can set one up.

## Step 5: Write the readout (one page, every quarter)

This goes to your manager, the CMO, or the CEO. Make it the standard PMM update at quarterly business reviews, so it becomes routine rather than a one-off.

1. **Why it mattered:** The company goal it supported.
2. **What we set out to change:** One sentence, plus the target.
3. **What happened:** The main number from start to now, whether you hit the target, and product usage.
4. **Proof:** Three log entries with links: one call, one deal, and one decision.
5. **How sure we are:** Whether this was a test group or a with/without comparison, and anything else that may have helped.
6. **What didn't work:** One honest miss and what you changed because of it.
7. **What's next:** The next target, or what you're stopping.

### Example readout

> **Why:** Supports the company goal of growing mid-market revenue 25% this year.
>
> **Goal:** Win more mid-market deals against Competitor X on reporting (31% → 40% by end of Q2).
>
> **Result:** 41% at day 90. Target hit. 33% of mid-market accounts now use the dashboard.
>
> **Proof:** (1) Acme call where the buyer asked for a second demo after hearing the new reporting line, $48K, won. (2) The "reporting gap" loss reason dropped from 4 deals to 1. (3) Product moved scheduled exports up a quarter after win/loss showed 6 losses worth $210K for the same reason.
>
> **How sure we are:** We rolled out to the East region first. East's win rate rose 10 points in that time; West rose 2. Competitor X also raised prices in March, which likely helped both.
>
> **Miss:** The West team was slow to use the message. A 20-minute retraining at day 30 fixed it.
>
> **Next:** Same approach for enterprise, with a target of 25% → 33% against Competitor Y.

## Common mistakes this skill prevents

- Reporting what you built ("12 assets, 3 trainings") instead of what changed.
- Choosing the target after the results come in, or choosing it alone.
- Claiming a shared number as fully PMM's, or presenting a link as proof.
- Logging only good news.
- Blaming the message when the product is the problem, or the reverse.
- Running projects that don't connect to a company goal.
- Waiting until review season to piece together evidence from memory.
