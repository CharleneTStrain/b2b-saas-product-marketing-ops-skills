# PMM impact tracker

**The problem:** When someone asks "how do you know it's working?", PMM reports what it built — assets, trainings — instead of what changed, and pieces the evidence together from memory at review time.

**What it does:** Sets a target before a project starts, logs signs it's landing each week, tracks whether reps and buyers repeat the new messaging, tags lost deals with their real reason and dollar value, compares deals that used an asset against deals that didn't, splits shared numbers honestly, scores launches at days 30, 60, and 90, and writes a one-page quarterly readout. Works for sales-led, self-serve, and mixed motions, and reports a link rather than proof unless you ran a holdout.

**Use it with:** a launch brief or project plan, CRM and deal data, call recordings, win/loss notes, product usage or funnel data, and your company goals.

**You get:** an impact plan, a weekly movement log, a monthly launch scorecard, and a quarterly one-page impact story with three changes and the proof for each.
