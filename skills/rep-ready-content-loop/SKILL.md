# Rep-Ready Content Loop

**What it does:** It makes sure every sales asset is built for a real moment in a deal, sits where reps already work, and stays accurate, so reps actually use it and it helps win deals.

**When to use it:**
- **Full version:** Battlecards, objection guides, talk tracks, demo scripts, one-pagers, pricing guides, case studies, ROI tools, and launch kits.
- **Light version:** A small update to an existing asset. Use the card builder, rep voice rewrite, and content finder audit only.
- **CS and support content:** Customer enablement one-pagers, onboarding guides, renewal talk tracks, and support macros. Every file has a CS version; see the mapping below.
- **Skip it:** Content that isn't used in a live conversation with a buyer or customer, such as blog posts or awareness campaigns.

This skill works inside whatever tools you already have, such as your CRM, call recorder, Slack, or sales content tool. It sets what goes in them and how they're kept up to date; it doesn't replace them.

If your team has its own rules for sources or claims, use them alongside this skill.

## Using it for CS and support content

The same loop works for content CS managers and support teams use with existing customers. These are the differences:

| Step | Sales version | CS and support version |
|---|---|---|
| Ask | Lost deals, by moment and dollar value; reps, including a top performer and a new rep | Churned or downgraded accounts, ticket volume by topic, and failed setups; CS managers and agents, including one with the hardest accounts and one who's newer |
| Build | "When they say / You say" cards | "When the customer asks / Here's how" cards, with steps and when to escalate |
| Place | CRM, call recorder, Slack | Help desk or ticketing tool, CS platform, saved replies or macros, help center |
| Measure | Win rate in deals that used it | Resolution time, repeat tickets, setup completion, renewal or expansion in accounts that used it |
| Partner | Sales leader | CS or support leader |

The rest carries over unchanged: the product accuracy check, the refresh cadence, the 90-day retire rule, the stop rule, and closing the loop on every request.

## Ground rules across every file

- **Start from the moments costing the most,** not from a list of assets.
- **One moment per card, one home per asset.**
- **No card ships** without a rep voice check and a product accuracy check.
- **Low use means check placement first,** before rewriting.
- **Lead with deal results,** not opens and sends.
- **Sales leadership owns adoption;** PMM owns the content.
- **Close the loop** on every rep request.

## Common mistakes this skill prevents

- Building content for what marketing wants to say instead of the moments that are losing deals.
- Interviewing reps for every single asset instead of once per topic.
- Putting content in a library reps have to leave their workflow to find.
- Packing every fact onto one card until nobody can scan it.
- Shipping talk tracks that don't sound like a rep, or rewrites that overpromise the product.
- Letting competitive content or product claims go stale.
- Rewriting content that's unused because nobody can find it.
- Reporting opens and sends instead of deals.
- Keeping every asset forever, or building more of a type that never gets used.
- Shipping a launch as a folder of ten PDFs.
- Sending Product one-off feature requests instead of grouped patterns.
- Asking reps for feedback and never telling them what changed.
- Making PMM solely responsible for whether reps use the content.
