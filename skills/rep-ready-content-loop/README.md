# Rep-ready content loop

**The problem:** PMM builds battlecards, one-pagers, and talk tracks that reps don't use, because they're built for what marketing wants to say, live somewhere reps have to go looking, and go stale.

**What it does:** Starts from the deal moments costing the most, audits where content lives and moves the most-used pieces into reps' own tools, turns long assets into cards a rep can scan in 10 seconds, has a top rep rewrite them in their own words, keeps every asset owned and fresh, runs a monthly field feedback loop, judges content by deal results, ships one launch-day sales kit per launch, and shares adoption ownership with sales leadership. Has a CS and support version of every step.

**Use it with:** existing sales content, rep interviews, call recordings, CRM deal data, and the tools reps already work in.

**You get:** 10-second cards in reps' tools, a freshness schedule with an owner on every asset, a launch-day sales kit, and an adoption scorecard PMM and Sales both watch.
