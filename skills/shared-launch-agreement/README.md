# Shared launch agreement

**The problem:** Product, Sales, and Marketing run three launch plans that only meet on launch day, so the website, deck, demo, and help docs each describe the product differently, decisions get reopened in week ten, and launches stretch to 12–16 weeks.

**What it does:** Maps one launch process the three teams share, fills in a one-page agreement at the product kickoff, tests the positioning against real deals with all three teams' leaders, builds every asset from one claim source list with a named product owner on each claim, logs decisions and settles ties on a clock, checks every asset against the others at day 30, and cuts the waiting time that stretches launches. Includes a minimum version, an async path, and a business case for leadership.

**Use it with:** a product brief or kickoff notes, real deals and call recordings, the current website, deck, demo, and help docs, and your past launch timelines.

**You get:** a kickoff agreement, signed-off positioning, a claim source list every asset pulls from, a decision record, a day-30 consistency check, and a launch timeline review.
