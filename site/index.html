Sheet 02 · How it worksClick any piece to open it

Agree. Build. Prove.

Run pmm-ops-context once. It writes down your product, buyers, how it's sold, the leaders who sign off, launch tiers, and where claims and sales content live. Then the three skills run in order: agree on the story before anything is built, build what reps use from that story, then prove what changed.

Sheet 03 · RulesBuilt into every skill

What every skill refuses to do

The same four rules run through the whole library, so a launch story, a battlecard, or an impact readout holds up when someone checks it.

Rule 1

Agree on the target before anything is built.

Nothing gets measured or shipped that wasn't planned first.

Rule 2

Every claim has proof and a product owner.

Someone confirmed it's true today. Nobody writes claims from memory.

Rule 3

Unknowns stay open.

Each one gets an owner and a date instead of a guess.

Rule 4

A link is reported as a link.

Not as proof. And a shared number is never PMM's alone.
