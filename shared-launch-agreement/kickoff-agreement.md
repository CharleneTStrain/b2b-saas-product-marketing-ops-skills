# Kickoff Agreement

Part of the [shared launch agreement](SKILL.md). Step 1: agree on the basics at the product kickoff, before anything is built.

Find the meeting that already happens every time Product hands off what's being built, usually the kickoff after design and before build. Everything marketing and sales need is decided by then, and nothing is built yet, so answers are still cheap. Fill in one page there with all three teams in the room.

---

## Steps

### 1. Find the meeting

Ask a PM: "What meeting happens every time, after design and before build?" If none exists, ask for 20 minutes at the end of the next sprint planning or design review where this feature comes up. Don't create a new standing meeting.

### 2. Pre-fill before the meeting

PMM fills in what it can from the product spec, so the meeting confirms instead of starting from blank. Mark anything you guessed as "To confirm."

### 3. Confirm it in the room (20 minutes, the PM's main time ask)

Walk through the page with the PM, a sales leader or senior rep, and PMM. Every field ends as **Agreed** or **Open**, with an owner and a date.

### 4. Send it the same day

Post the agreement where the launch team works, and link it from the decision record.

---

## Template: kickoff agreement

| Field | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | The name and one line on what it is | New reporting dashboard |
| Tier | 1, 2, or 3, and who set it | Tier 1 (PMM, agreed with Product) |
| What it does | One or two sentences, in plain words | Builds a shareable report from your account data in about five minutes, with no setup |
| Who it's for | The audience, as specifically as you can | Mid-market ops managers who build weekly reports by hand |
| Problem it solves | What they struggle with today | Reports take half a day each week, and still don't match what leadership sees |
| What it doesn't do yet | Limits reps must not promise | No custom formulas; no scheduled email delivery |
| Main claim | The one thing we want buyers to believe | Reporting in five minutes, not half a day |
| Proof we have, or will have | Where the evidence comes from | Beta: 12 accounts, median build time 6 minutes (from usage data) |
| Ship date | Date, and whether it's firm | June 3, tentative until May 20 |
| Pricing or packaging change? | Yes or no, and who decides | No; included in Pro and above |
| Who's affected | Teams that need to know before launch | Sales, CS, support, docs |
| Open questions | Each with an owner and a decide-by date | Name the feature? PMM, by May 6 |
| Status | Agreed (by name) / Open | Agreed: [PM], [sales leader], [PMM] |

---

## Rules for the kickoff agreement

- **"What it doesn't do yet" is required.** If Product can't list a single limit, ask again; there's always one.
- **"What it doesn't do yet" is never a promise to build it.** It only tells reps what not to promise. Nothing on it goes to buyers, and nothing on it gets a date. Say this in the room, so PMs can list limits without negotiating the roadmap.
- **One main claim.** If the room can't pick one, the audience isn't clear yet.
- **Every open question gets an owner and a decide-by date** that falls before the positioning sign-off.
- **Write it in plain words.** If a rep couldn't read it out loud to a buyer, rewrite it.
- **Agreed means named.** "We discussed it" isn't agreement.
- **If something changes,** update the page and log it in the [decision record](decision-record.md). Don't fork a second version.

---

## After the kickoff

- **Main claim, audience, and proof** go into the [claim source list](claim-source-list.md) as "To confirm" until the [positioning sign-off](positioning-sign-off.md).
- **"What it doesn't do yet"** becomes the "What's not ready" section of the [launch-day sales kit](../rep-ready-content-loop/launch-day-sales-kit.md).
- **The main claim and audience** feed the "change we want" and "who has to change" fields in the [impact plan](../pmm-impact-tracker/impact-plan.md).
