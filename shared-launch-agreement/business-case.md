# Business Case

Part of the [shared launch agreement](SKILL.md). Shows leadership what slow, misaligned launches cost today, and what fixing them is worth, before you ask anyone to change how they work.

Use it to propose the skill, and again after the first few launches to show whether it worked. Build it from your own launches, not industry averages. A leader will trust one real timeline from their own company over any benchmark.

---

## Steps

### 1. Pick your last two or three Tier 1 launches

Use the [launch timeline review](launch-timeline-review.md) to rebuild each one: weeks from kickoff to launch, and how many of those days were waiting.

### 2. Count four costs

| Cost | How to count it | Where to find it |
|---|---|---|
| Weeks lost to waiting | Waiting days from the timeline review, per launch | Timeline review |
| Rework | Hours spent redoing assets after a late change, per launch | Ask the asset owners; an estimate is fine if you say so |
| Revenue delayed | Expected new pipeline or revenue per month after launch, times the weeks lost | The launch's target in the [impact plan](../pmm-impact-tracker/impact-plan.md), or the product team's forecast |
| Contradictions buyers saw | Gaps found in the [consistency check](consistency-check.md), or examples from lost deals and support tickets | Consistency check, win/loss notes |

If a number doesn't exist yet, write "Not tracked" rather than guessing. One solid number is more convincing than four soft ones.

### 3. Set the target

Agree one target with leadership: a launch length (for example, Tier 1 from 14 weeks to 9 within three launches) and what it's worth (the revenue that arrives earlier).

### 4. Name the ask

Ask for the smallest thing: a trial of the minimum version on the next launch, with a review after it ships.

### 5. Report back after each launch

Update the same page with the new timeline, rework hours, and gaps found. Keep the original numbers so the comparison stays visible.

## Template: business case

```
SHARED LAUNCH AGREEMENT · Prepared by: [PMM name] · Date: [date]

TODAY (last [2–3] Tier 1 launches):
  Average kickoff to launch: __ weeks
  Average days waiting: __ (__% of the total)
  Biggest wait: ___
  Rework per launch: __ hours (counted / estimated)
  Contradictions buyers saw: ___ (example: ___)

WHAT IT COSTS:
  Revenue delayed per launch: $__ per month × __ weeks lost = $__
  (source: ___)

THE TARGET:
  Tier 1 launches from __ weeks to __ weeks, within __ launches
  Worth: $__ in revenue arriving earlier per launch

THE ASK:
  Try the minimum version (kickoff agreement + claim source list) on [next launch].
  Time it needs: PM about 45 minutes; PMM 3–4 hours; no new meetings.
  Review it with you on [date, about 45 days after launch].

AFTER EACH LAUNCH:
  | Launch | Weeks | Days waiting | Rework hours | Gaps found | vs. target |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
```

---

## Rules

- **Use your own launches,** not benchmarks.
- **Say which numbers are estimates.** An honest estimate holds up better than a precise-looking guess.
- **Ask for a trial,** not a rollout.
- **Report back after every launch,** including the ones that didn't improve.
- **Keep it to one page.**
