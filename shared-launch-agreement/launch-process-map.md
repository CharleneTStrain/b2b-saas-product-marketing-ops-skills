# Launch Process Map

Part of the [shared launch agreement](SKILL.md). Step 0: write down one launch process all three teams share. Do this once, then reuse it for every launch.

Most companies don't have one launch process. They have three: Product's release plan, Sales' enablement plan, and Marketing's campaign plan, which only meet on launch day. This file turns them into one page everyone agrees to.

---

## Steps

### 1. Collect what each team does today (one hour)

Ask one person from each team to walk through their last Tier 1 launch, step by step. Don't ask what the process should be; ask what actually happened.

**Questions to ask:**
1. "When did you first hear about this launch, and from whom?"
2. "What did you need from the other teams, and when did you get it?"
3. "Where did you wait on someone?"
4. "What did you redo, and why?"
5. "Who decided the name, the price, and the audience? When?"
6. "When did you consider your part ready?"

### 2. Lay out one shared timeline

Put every team's steps on one timeline, using weeks from the product kickoff. Mark three things:
- **Handoffs:** where one team waits on another.
- **Decisions:** where something gets locked (name, audience, price, date).
- **Gaps:** where two teams thought the other one owned something.

### 3. Agree who decides what

| Decision | Decides | Must agree | Told after | Locked by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What ships, and what doesn't yet | Product | PMM | Sales | Kickoff |
| Ship date | Product | PMM, Sales | Everyone else | Kickoff (tentative), 2 weeks before launch (final) |
| Launch tier | PMM | Product | Sales, Marketing | Kickoff |
| Audience | PMM | Product, Sales | Everyone else | Positioning sign-off |
| Main claim and positioning | PMM | Product, Sales | Everyone else | Positioning sign-off |
| Name | PMM | Product | Sales | Positioning sign-off |
| Price and packaging | [your pricing owner] | Product, Sales, PMM | Everyone else | Positioning sign-off |
| Sales readiness | Sales leader | PMM | Everyone else | 1 week before launch |

Adjust the names to your company. The point is that each decision has one decider, and everyone knows when it locks. Read "Sales" as the Conversion seat (see "Who fills each seat" in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md)).

**If another doc already covers this** (a project workplan, or a decision framework that names who decides and approves), link it instead of copying it, and use this map only for what that doc leaves out. **If a small cross-team group runs the launch** (a tiger team or squad), name the group's members against the three seats, so each decision still has one decider.

### 4. Define "ready" for each team

| Team | Ready means | Checked by | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product | Feature works for the stated audience; "What's not ready" list is final | PMM | 2 weeks before launch |
| Sales | Reps watched the training; the kit is in their tools; the sales leader agrees reps can pitch it | Sales leader | 1 week before launch |
| Marketing | Website, email, and in-app copy pull from the claim source list; every claim is confirmed | PMM | 1 week before launch |
| CS and support | Help docs are live; agents know the top two customer questions | CS or support lead | Launch day |

### 5. Get sign-off once

Share the one-page map with the Product, Sales, and Marketing leaders. Each leader signs off by name. Pin it where launches are planned.

## Template: launch process map

```
LAUNCH PROCESS · Last updated: [date] · Owner: [PMM name]
SIGNED OFF BY: Product [name, date] · Sales [name, date] · Marketing [name, date]
TARGET: Tier 1 launches in [__] weeks from kickoff (today: [__] weeks)

TIMELINE (weeks from product kickoff):
  Week 0: Product kickoff + kickoff agreement
  Week [_]: Positioning sign-off
  Week [_]: Claim source list complete; assets start
  Week [_]: Product ready check
  Week [_]: Sales ready check
  Launch: [day of week]
  Day 30: Consistency check
  Day 45: Timeline review

WHO DECIDES WHAT: [table from step 3]

WHAT "READY" MEANS: [table from step 4]

TIE-BREAKS: see tie-break rule
```

---

## Rules

- **Map what actually happened,** not the process on the wiki.
- **One decider per decision.** "Must agree" means they can block, not that they decide.
- **Every handoff has a date.** "When it's ready" isn't a date.
- **Start from week 0 at the product kickoff,** not from the launch date. Working backward from a fixed date is how marketing ends up with two weeks.
- **Revisit it** after every timeline review, or when the team structure changes.
